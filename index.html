A structured, W-2 workforce restoring regional watersheds and reducing hazardous fuels, while building career-track skills in ecology, native plant propagation, and forestry. Integrating FAA Part 107 GIS thermal mapping and imaging for assessing plantings and soil conditions.

A Legacy of Service, Stability, and Land Stewardship

Operating out of our centralized headquarters in Linn County, Oregon, under watershipstewardsempowering.org, our mission bridges the gap between veteran vocational rehabilitation and critical regional land stewardship. Through strict morning musters, asset-light operational frameworks, and robust partnerships, we turn wilderness defense into stable civilian pathways.

Through our fully integrated programs—uniting veteran-led field teams with agricultural diagnostics and open community training—we do more than execute daily field missions. We are establishing stable, respected land stewardship careers that allow our participants to anchor themselves as key community assets and stable landowners. This is a complete transition from initial mission to permanent stewardship, actively building a lasting legacy of healthier ecosystems and a thriving, self-reliant future that our children and grandchildren can look back on with pride.
