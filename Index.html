Forest & Watershed Restoration

Structured veteran crews conduct intensive, labor-intensive hazardous fuels reduction, manual slash piling, hand-line construction, and post-wildfire reforestation to enhance regional ecosystem health.

Native Plant Nursery & Propagation

Scaling up capacity for regional riparian and habitat recovery projects. We cultivate a wide variety of native Willamette Valley species for restoration efforts and community distribution.

Academic Research & Aerial Mapping

Comprehensive training track for FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot licensing, utilizing aerial thermal imaging for terrain assessment, post-fire watershed analysis, and vegetation health monitoring.

Operational Framework & Training

A fully integrated, W-2 workforce model combining structured field operations with formal, verifiable educational pathways in soil science, botany, and pest management.

A Legacy of Service, Stability, and Land Stewardship

Operating out of our centralized headquarters in Linn County, Oregon, under watershipstewardsempowering.org, our mission bridges the gap between veteran vocational rehabilitation and critical regional land stewardship. Through strict morning musters, asset-light operational frameworks, and robust partnerships, we turn wilderness defense into stable civilian careers that build lasting community assets.

This represents a complete transition from initial mission to permanent stewardship—ensuring healthier regional ecosystems, sustainable agricultural optimization, and a thriving, self-reliant future that our children and grandchildren can look back on with pride.

0800 Safety Musters

Structured military-style accountability, real-time hazard briefs, and rigorous daily standard operating procedures.

Asset-Light Mandate

Zero early equipment debt; heavy field operations integrated seamlessly via direct timber industry subcontracts.
